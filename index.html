Abstract

Video diffusion transformers rely on 3D full attention over tens of thousands of spatiotemporal tokens, and its quadratic cost dominates inference latency. Sparse attention reduces this cost, but existing methods face a dilemma: static methods apply predefined masks and cannot capture input-dependent attention, whereas dynamic methods reconstruct the sparse mask repeatedly, paying online to rediscover positional structure that is largely stable across inputs.

We propose PoCoAttn, a training-free sparse attention framework that separates input-stable from input-dependent attention and resolves the former offline and the latter online. Across four video diffusion models and three tasks, PoCoAttn consistently achieves a better speed–fidelity Pareto frontier than state-of-the-art (SOTA) methods, improving PSNR by 1.9 to 5.0 dB over baselines and delivering up to 4.1× attention and 2.0× denoising speedups over dense FlashAttention-3 attention.
